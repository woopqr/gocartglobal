Editorial policy

Observed facts vs. judgment

Ratings, review counts, fetched prices and distances are observed values confirmed in the source response. Recommended rankings and per-traveler suitability are editorial judgments made according to our published calculation criteria.

Disclosing limits

Where a small review sample, an unconfirmed price or missing amenity data affects how a result should be read, we flag it near the relevant content. We do not invent unverified strengths or experiences.

Affiliate relationships

Some booking links are Agoda affiliate links, and we may earn a commission when a booking occurs. Affiliate status does not change what data is included or excluded, or how rankings are calculated.

Updates & corrections

We treat prices and availability as changeable information. When a data error is confirmed, we correct both the source data and the generated documents, and reflect material changes in judgment on the revision date.
