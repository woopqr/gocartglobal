Bali Wellness & Spa — The Finest Five-Star Stays

Wellness in Bali goes deeper than the spa menu. Ubud is the center of gravity, with retreats set above the Ayung River offering yoga, meditation, breathwork and plant-based cuisine. Traditional Balinese massage, flower baths and boreh scrubs made with local spices are rooted in the island's healing heritage. For a true reset, choose a multi-day program with consultations and daily treatments, then balance it with dawn walks through the rice fields.
