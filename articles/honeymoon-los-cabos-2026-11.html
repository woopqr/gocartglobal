Why Los Cabos for a Honeymoon trip

Los Cabos suits honeymooners who want romance with a sense of adventure. Book a cliffside villa with a private plunge pool on the Tourist Corridor, overlooking the Sea of Cortez. Take a private sunset sail around the arch at Land's End, swim and snorkel in the protected bays such as Santa Maria and Chileno, and spend an afternoon in the art galleries of San José del Cabo. Ask for a private beach dinner, and keep in mind that many beaches here are too rough for swimming.

At the southern tip of the Baja California peninsula, where the desert meets the Sea of Cortez and the Pacific, Los Cabos delivers a distinctive mix of rugged landscapes, golf, sportfishing and polished resort luxury. Its top properties feature cliffside villas, infinity pools and spas facing the dramatic coastline, appealing to travelers who want sun, scenery and outdoor adventure.

When to go: November through April is the most pleasant time, with warm, dry days and cooler evenings; it also coincides with humpback whale season, roughly December through March. Summers are hot and humid, and the Pacific hurricane season runs from roughly June through November.

Cabo San LucasThe lively marina town near the famous rock arch at Land's End is the base for sportfishing, boating and nightlife, ideal for those who want energy and easy access to the water.
The Tourist CorridorThe coastal stretch between Cabo San Lucas and San José del Cabo holds many of the region's top resorts and golf courses, perfect for travelers who want seclusion and space.
San José del CaboThe quieter colonial town features art galleries and a charming historic center, suited to those who want culture, dining and a more relaxed pace.
