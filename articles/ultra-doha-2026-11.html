Why Doha for a Ultra-Luxury trip

Doha's highest tier offers palatial suites and private villas where Arabian hospitality is taken to its fullest: dedicated butlers, private dining with Gulf specialties and chauffeured cars on call. Arrange private after-hours museum experiences, a desert camp in the dunes near the Inland Sea, or a yacht along the coast. Discretion, space and attentive service are the signatures of the city's most exclusive stays.

Qatar's capital pairs a striking modern skyline with deep Arabian heritage, from the Museum of Islamic Art on its own island to the desert-rose architecture of the National Museum of Qatar and the lantern-lit lanes of Souq Waqif. Its luxury is polished and serene: high-rise suites in West Bay, waterfront living on The Pearl-Qatar, and hospitality steeped in Gulf tradition.

When to go: November to March is the best time to visit, with warm, sunny days and cool evenings ideal for the corniche and desert. June to September is extremely hot and humid, making indoor attractions and spas the focus.
