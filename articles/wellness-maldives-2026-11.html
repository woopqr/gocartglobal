Why Maldives for a Wellness & Spa trip

Wellness in the Maldives is built around the ocean. Many resorts have overwater spas with glass floors, yoga pavilions facing the sunrise, and programs that combine treatments with nutrition, meditation and sleep coaching. Daily snorkeling, paddleboarding and swimming in warm, clear water do the rest. Choose a remote atoll for a digital detox and a resort with a dedicated wellness program for a structured reset.

The Maldives is the benchmark for overwater luxury: more than a thousand coral islands spread across a chain of atolls in the Indian Ocean, most hosting a single resort. Expect villas over turquoise lagoons, house reefs alive with turtles and reef sharks, and seaplane transfers. It does seclusion, barefoot elegance and marine life better than almost anywhere on earth.

When to go: December to April is the dry northeast monsoon season, with calm seas, sunshine and low humidity. May to November is wetter and windier, though it brings manta ray and whale shark sightings and quieter resorts.
