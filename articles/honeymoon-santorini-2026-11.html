Why Santorini for a Honeymoon trip

Santorini is arguably the world's most classic honeymoon setting, and the key is the suite itself. Choose a cave suite in Imerovigli or Oia with a heated private plunge pool on the caldera edge, so sunset can be watched in privacy rather than among crowds. Spend a day sailing the caldera on a private catamaran, swimming near the hot springs, and save an evening for a wine tasting at one of the island's cliffside wineries, where the volcanic Assyrtiko is the local star.

Santorini's luxury is built into the cliffs: whitewashed cave suites carved into the rim of a flooded volcanic caldera, each with a private terrace and often a plunge pool hanging above the Aegean. Few places on earth offer sunsets this dramatic from your own room, and the island's volcanic wines, black-sand beaches and quiet villages round out a stay devoted to views and privacy.

When to go: Late May, June and September into early October offer warm, sunny days, warm seas and fewer crowds than July and August, when the island is at its busiest and the meltemi winds can blow strongly. Many hotels close from roughly November through March.

OiaThe island's most photographed village, at the northern tip of the caldera, is famed for its sunsets and suits couples who want the iconic blue-domed setting and are happy to share the lanes at dusk.
ImerovigliPerched at the highest point of the caldera rim, it is quieter than Oia with equally sweeping views, ideal for honeymooners who prioritize privacy and a calm terrace.
Fira and FirostefaniThe island's lively main town and its gentler neighbor offer caldera views with easy access to restaurants, shops and transport, a good fit for travelers who want convenience.
