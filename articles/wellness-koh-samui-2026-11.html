Why Koh Samui for a Wellness & Spa trip

Samui has a long-standing reputation as a wellness island, with retreats offering detox programs, fasting, yoga and holistic therapies. Many luxury spas are set on hillsides with treatment rooms facing the sea, offering Thai massage, herbal steam and coconut-based rituals. A reset here pairs well with slow island living: morning yoga, nutritious Thai cuisine, beach walks and early nights.

Set in the Gulf of Thailand, Koh Samui is a hilly, coconut-palm island ringed by soft-sand beaches and quiet bays. Its luxury leans intimate and relaxed: hillside villas with sweeping sea views, beachfront resorts on gentle bays, and easy access to the protected islands of Ang Thong Marine National Park.

When to go: January to April is typically the sunniest, driest stretch, and the island often stays pleasant through the summer months when Thailand's west coast is rainy. The heaviest rain usually falls from October to December, during the Gulf's northeast monsoon.
