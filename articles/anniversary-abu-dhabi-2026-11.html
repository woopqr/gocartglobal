Why Abu Dhabi for a Anniversary Escape trip

Abu Dhabi offers an anniversary with gravitas. Visit the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in the late afternoon, when the white marble shifts color with the setting sun, then spend an unhurried morning at the Louvre Abu Dhabi beneath its latticed dome. Settle into a grand suite on the Corniche or a beachfront suite on Saadiyat, and finish with a celebratory dinner or a private evening in the desert.

The UAE's capital offers a calmer, more cultural counterpoint to Dubai, anchored by the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Louvre Abu Dhabi and the presidential palace Qasr Al Watan. Its luxury is spacious and stately: palatial hotels along the Corniche, beach resorts on Saadiyat Island beside protected dunes, and remote retreats among the towering sands of the Empty Quarter.

When to go: November to March brings warm, sunny days and pleasant evenings, perfect for beaches, golf and desert excursions. June to September is intensely hot and humid, best suited to indoor culture and pool time.

Saadiyat IslandA cultural district and natural beach home to the Louvre Abu Dhabi, ideal for couples and families who want sand and museums together.
The CornicheThe city's waterfront boulevard, lined with grand hotels and skyline views, suited to first-timers and classic luxury seekers.
Yas IslandAn entertainment hub with the Yas Marina Circuit, theme parks and golf, a strong pick for families and thrill-seekers.
