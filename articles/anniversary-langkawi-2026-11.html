Why Langkawi for a Anniversary Escape trip

For a milestone, Langkawi offers celebration with a sense of discovery. Charter a yacht for a sunset sail past the limestone islets of the southern archipelago, with a stop at the Lake of the Pregnant Maiden on Pulau Dayang Bunting. Choose a suite with sea views, plan a Malay feast or a beachfront dinner, and use the island's duty-free status to choose a meaningful gift.

Langkawi is an archipelago of roughly a hundred islands off Malaysia's northwest coast, designated a UNESCO Global Geopark for its ancient rock formations, primary rainforest and mangrove forests. Luxury here is nature-first: resorts set within old-growth jungle above secluded bays, wildlife on the doorstep, and spas that draw on Malay herbal traditions. The main island is also duty-free.

When to go: December to March is the driest and sunniest period, with calm seas ideal for beach days and boat trips. From roughly April to November conditions are wetter, with September and October usually the rainiest months.
