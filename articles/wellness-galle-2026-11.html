Why Galle for a Wellness & Spa trip

Sri Lanka has a long Ayurvedic tradition, and the Galle coast is one of the best places to experience it properly. Retreats here typically begin with a consultation, then build a program of oil treatments, herbal remedies, yoga, meditation and a tailored diet. Choose a property in the green hinterland for quiet, or on the beach for ocean swims between sessions. A stay of a week or longer lets a traditional program have its full effect.

Galle sits on Sri Lanka's southwest coast, where a fort first built by the Portuguese and later expanded by the Dutch encloses a town of colonial villas, churches and lanes of boutiques. Luxury stays range from restored mansions inside the ramparts to beachfront villas along the southern coast. The area excels at heritage, Ayurveda and easygoing beach luxury, with tea country and national parks within reach.

When to go: December to April is the dry season on the southwest coast, with sunny skies and calm seas ideal for swimming. From May to September the southwest monsoon brings rain and rough surf, though the landscape is greener and the coast far quieter.
