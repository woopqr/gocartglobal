Why Krabi for a Honeymoon trip

Krabi's honeymoons are cinematic. Arrive by longtail boat to a beach backed by limestone cliffs on Railay, or settle into a pool villa on the quiet Tubkaek coast facing the Hong Islands. Charter a private boat at dawn to beat day-trippers to secluded lagoons, then return for a candlelit dinner on the sand. Kayaking through mangroves and cliffside sunsets add adventure without sacrificing romance.

Krabi is defined by its towering limestone karsts, emerald water and island-dotted Andaman coastline. Luxury here is about setting: resorts reached by longtail boat, beachfront villas facing sheer cliffs, and easy access to the Phi Phi, Hong and Poda islands. It suits travelers who prize scenery and calm over nightlife.

When to go: November to April is the dry season, with clear skies, calm seas and the best conditions for island hopping and climbing. The southwest monsoon from May to October brings rain and rougher water, which can limit some boat trips.

RailayA peninsula cut off by cliffs and reachable only by boat, with dramatic beaches that suit adventurous couples and climbers.
TubkaekA quiet stretch of coast facing the Hong Islands, home to secluded resorts ideal for travelers seeking total calm.
