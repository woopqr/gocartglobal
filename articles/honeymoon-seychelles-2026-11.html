Why Seychelles for a Honeymoon trip

Seychelles honeymoons are about having a beach almost to yourselves. Choose a pool villa tucked among granite boulders, then spend a day on La Digue, cycling to Anse Source d'Argent with its sculpted rocks and shallow turquoise water. Walk the Vallée de Mai on Praslin, where the coco de mer palm grows wild, and snorkel with hawksbill turtles. Ending the trip with a few nights on a private island gives couples complete privacy.

The Seychelles is an Indian Ocean archipelago known for smooth granite boulders, powder-white coves and some of the most protected forests and reefs in the region. Luxury here ranges from hillside resorts on Mahé and Praslin to private islands with only a few villas. It excels at nature-led seclusion: giant tortoises, rare birds, empty beaches and water clear enough to see turtles from the shore.

When to go: April to May and October to November are the calmest periods, with light winds, warm water and the best visibility for snorkeling and diving. December to March is hotter and wetter, while June to September brings steadier southeast trade winds and choppier seas on exposed coasts.

MahéThe largest island, with the international airport and the small capital of Victoria, offers mountain-backed beaches and the widest choice of resorts.
Praslin and La DiguePraslin's Vallée de Mai forest and quiet bays suit slower travelers, with La Digue's famous boulder beaches a short ferry ride away.
