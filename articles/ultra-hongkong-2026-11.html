Why Hong Kong for a Ultra-Luxury trip

The pinnacle of Hong Kong luxury lies in vast harbor-view suites, often with a private terrace or a dining room for entertaining. Expect chauffeured airport transfers, butlers, private shopping appointments in Central's flagship boutiques and some of Asia's most acclaimed Cantonese and French restaurants without leaving the building. The finest addresses are in Central, Admiralty and on the Kowloon waterfront, where the harbor fills every window and the skyline becomes the room's main artwork after dark.

Hong Kong pairs one of the world's most dramatic harbors with a long tradition of grand, service-driven hotels. Luxury here means harbor-view suites, Cantonese fine dining, afternoon tea and polished shopping malls connected by covered walkways. Stays cluster on both sides of Victoria Harbour, with Hong Kong Island's skyline and Kowloon's waterfront promenade facing each other across the water.

When to go: October to December is the most comfortable period, with cooler temperatures, low humidity and clear skies. Summers are hot, humid and rainy, and the typhoon season runs roughly from May to October.
