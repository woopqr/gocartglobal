Why Dubai for a Ultra-Luxury trip

Dubai's top tier is a world of superlatives you can actually book: multi-bedroom villas on Palm Jumeirah with private beaches and pools, suites with round-the-clock butlers, and desert estates where the only neighbors are oryx. Arrive by private jet, move between hotels by chauffeured car or helicopter, and keep a crewed yacht on standby in the marina. Personal shoppers and private dining rooms complete the experience.

Dubai sits where the Arabian Desert meets the warm waters of the Gulf, its skyline crowned by the Burj Khalifa. Its luxury is unapologetically grand: palatial beach resorts on Palm Jumeirah, sky-high suites downtown, secluded desert retreats, and a restaurant scene shaped by chefs from around the world. Shopping, golf and private yachts round out a city built for indulgence.

When to go: November to March is the ideal window, with warm, sunny days and comfortable evenings for dining outdoors and desert trips. June to September is extremely hot and humid, best suited to indoor and poolside stays.
