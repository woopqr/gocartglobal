Why Honolulu for a Honeymoon trip

For a honeymoon on Oahu, balance Waikiki's buzz with seclusion. Choose an oceanfront suite with a lanai facing Diamond Head, or retreat to a quieter beachfront resort in Kahala or Ko Olina. Book a sunset catamaran sail off Waikiki, hike to the Diamond Head summit at dawn, and drive the windward coast to Lanikai and Kailua beaches. A private helicopter tour over the island's waterfalls and green ridges makes an unforgettable shared memory.

Oahu combines the energy of Waikiki with the island's quieter shores. Luxury here means oceanfront suites facing Diamond Head, beachfront resorts on the calm lagoons of the leeward coast, and the gracious aloha style of Hawaiian hospitality. It suits travelers who want world-class dining, surfing, hiking and Pearl Harbor history alongside time on the sand.

When to go: Oahu is warm year-round, with the drier, sunnier months running roughly from April through October. Spring and fall are typically quieter and good value, while winter brings occasional showers and big surf to the North Shore.

WaikikiHonolulu's famous beachfront is the heart of Oahu's hotel scene, with Diamond Head views, high-end shopping and restaurants, ideal for those who want energy and convenience.
KahalaJust east of Diamond Head, this exclusive residential enclave offers a quiet beach and stately estates, perfect for travelers seeking calm within easy reach of Waikiki.
Ko OlinaOn Oahu's sunny west coast, this resort area features calm man-made lagoons and golf, a good choice for families and couples who want a self-contained resort feel.
