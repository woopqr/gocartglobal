Why Mauritius for a Ultra-Luxury trip

The island's top tier is made up of multi-bedroom private villas with infinity pools, gardens and dedicated staff, often set slightly apart from the main resort. Expect butlers, private chefs, yacht charters along the lagoon and helicopter transfers from the airport in the southeast, which spare you the long drive across the island. Stay on the east coast for golf and wide lagoon views, or in the southwest beneath Le Morne for mountain drama and seclusion.

Mauritius is a volcanic island in the Indian Ocean ringed by coral reefs, lagoons and white beaches, with mountains, sugarcane fields and rainforest inland. Its luxury resorts combine French, Indian and Creole influences in cuisine and hospitality, and the island offers more than beach time: championship golf, hiking, kitesurfing and a well-developed spa culture.

When to go: May to December brings cooler, drier weather, with September to November particularly pleasant. January to March is hot and humid with a risk of cyclones, while the east coast is breezier during the winter trade winds.
