Why New York for a Anniversary Escape trip

New York turns an anniversary into an event. Book a suite with Central Park views at a landmark hotel near Fifth Avenue, start with Champagne as the skyline lights up, and reserve a tasting menu at one of the city's acclaimed fine-dining rooms. A night at the Metropolitan Opera or a Broadway premiere, a horse-drawn carriage ride or a private stroll through the park, and a late-night jazz club in the Village give the celebration that only-in-New-York quality.

New York's luxury is all about access and energy: suites high above Central Park, a doorman who knows your name, and a short walk to Broadway, Fifth Avenue and the world's great museums. The city's best hotels range from storied grand dames on the Upper East Side to sleek downtown towers, all set against one of the most dynamic dining scenes anywhere.

When to go: September through November brings crisp weather and autumn color in Central Park, while April through June offers mild days and blooming parks. December is cold but festive, with holiday windows and lights; July and August can be hot and humid.
