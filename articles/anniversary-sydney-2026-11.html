Why Sydney for a Anniversary Escape trip

Celebrate an anniversary in Sydney with a harbor-view suite at The Rocks or Circular Quay, where the Opera House sails and the Harbour Bridge fill the windows. Book a tasting menu at one of the city's acclaimed restaurants, take in a performance at the Opera House, and climb the Harbour Bridge together at twilight. Spend a day in the Hunter Valley wine region, a couple of hours north, then return for cocktails on the water as the ferries criss-cross the harbor.

Sydney offers big-city sophistication framed by one of the world's most beautiful harbors. Luxury here means suites with views of the Sydney Opera House and Harbour Bridge, waterfront dining with world-class seafood and Australian wines, and easy access to the beaches of the eastern suburbs. It appeals to travelers who want an outdoor lifestyle with the culture and polish of a global city.

When to go: September through November and March through May offer mild, sunny weather ideal for exploring the harbor and beaches. December through February is summer, hot and busy, while June through August is cooler but often bright and clear.

The Rocks and Circular QuayThe historic harborfront district beside the Opera House and Harbour Bridge offers the city's most iconic views, perfect for first-time visitors and couples.
Sydney CBDThe central business district offers high-rise luxury hotels with skyline and harbor views, fine-dining restaurants and designer shopping, ideal for those who want convenience.
Double BayThis upscale harbor-side suburb in the eastern suburbs features chic boutiques and cafés, suited to travelers who want a quieter, residential feel within reach of the beaches.
