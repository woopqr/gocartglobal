Why Bali for a Anniversary Escape trip

For a milestone, Bali rewards ceremony. Plan a dinner timed to sunset at a clifftop restaurant in Uluwatu, or a private table set among rice terraces near Ubud. A traditional water blessing at a temple spring, guided respectfully by a local priest, adds meaning beyond the usual celebration. Book a villa with a dedicated butler who can stage flower-strewn baths, Balinese dance performances or a quiet dinner on your own terrace.

Bali pairs dramatic natural settings with a deep-rooted Hindu culture that shapes daily life, from temple offerings to village ceremonies. Its luxury does its best work in private pool villas: clifftop compounds above the Indian Ocean, jungle pavilions over river gorges, and calm beachfront estates, with spa traditions and attentive service woven through every stay.

When to go: The dry season from April to October brings sunny days and lower humidity, with May, June and September offering the best balance of weather and smaller crowds. November to March is the wet season, with heavier afternoon downpours but greener landscapes and quieter resorts.
