Why Mauritius for a Luxury Golf trip

Mauritius is one of the Indian Ocean's leading golf destinations, with courses spread across the island. Serious golfers base themselves on the east coast near Belle Mare, within reach of the Île aux Cerfs Golf Club, an island course reached by boat and surrounded by lagoon. Others head to the southwest and west for courses set against mountains and sugarcane fields. The cooler months from May to November are ideal.

Mauritius is a volcanic island in the Indian Ocean ringed by coral reefs, lagoons and white beaches, with mountains, sugarcane fields and rainforest inland. Its luxury resorts combine French, Indian and Creole influences in cuisine and hospitality, and the island offers more than beach time: championship golf, hiking, kitesurfing and a well-developed spa culture.

When to go: May to December brings cooler, drier weather, with September to November particularly pleasant. January to March is hot and humid with a risk of cyclones, while the east coast is breezier during the winter trade winds.

East Coast (Belle Mare)Long beaches, sheltered lagoons and proximity to Île aux Cerfs make it the hub for golf and large luxury resorts.
