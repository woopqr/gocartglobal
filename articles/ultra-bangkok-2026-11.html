Why Bangkok for a Ultra-Luxury trip

Bangkok's grandest stays are sprawling riverside suites in heritage hotels, where generations of royalty and writers have checked in, and penthouse floors in central towers with panoramic city views. Butlers unpack, plan and anticipate. Arrange a private longtail through the Thonburi canals, an after-hours temple visit with a scholar guide, or a personal shopper for Thai silk, and let a chauffeur handle the notorious traffic.

Bangkok is one of Asia's great hotel cities, where grand riverside landmarks and sleek skyscraper hotels compete on service that is famously gracious. Luxury here means watching longtail boats on the Chao Phraya from your suite, exploring gilded temples, and dining at a restaurant scene that ranges from street food to acclaimed tasting menus.

When to go: November to February is the most comfortable time to visit, with cooler, drier weather. March to May is the hottest stretch, while June to October brings the rainy season, usually in the form of heavy afternoon showers.
